# Visual references

Layout comes from screenshots that were opened and recorded in `references/screenshots/LEDGER.md`. The working downloads from the search sit in `references/screenshots/gathered/`. A file there is not a ledger row.

Do not fetch the Wayback Machine or `archive.org`. Do not use `references/**/*.html` or `static/archive/` as a layout source. Those files can stay on disk. A page is not restyled until its own ledger row has a viewed image. A shot from another era or another page does not fill the gap.

The live 2026 site and the live Battle.net shop are Midnight and the current shop only.
